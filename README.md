### hey, I'm Monkey.D.Scraping 🐒

I build small data tools that do one boring job well: turning public pages into clean rows you can actually use.
No login, no subscription, no "book a demo". You pay per row, and a first test costs about a cent.

Everything runs on [Apify](https://apify.com/monkey.d.scraping), so you get CSV, JSON or Excel, an API,
and it plugs into n8n, Make, Zapier or your AI agent (MCP).

**What people use it for:** tracking brand mentions, reading comments at scale, checking what competitors run as ads, watching prices.
Or your boss wants "all the Telegram posts about us" in a spreadsheet by Friday. Yeah, that.
